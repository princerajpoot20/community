### Why someone is “active in 12m” but not “active in 6m”

The 6-month window only looks at roughly the last six months before the run. Anyone whose **only** qualifying commits/PRs/reviews fell in **months 7–12** of the 12-month window will flip from **active** (12m) to **inactive** (6m).
